Weighted probability

Each prototype gets a relative weight. Change the sliders to see its chance of being selected for an instance point.

P(i) = w(i) / Σ w(j)

Chance of prototype i = its weight ÷ sum of all weights

Weights

Selection chances

Think of raffle tickets

A weight of 6 versus three weights of 1 means 6 tickets out of 9, or 66.7%. Multiplying every weight by the same amount doesn't change the odds. Zero weight means a prototype is never selected.
