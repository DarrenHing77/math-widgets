Dot product and angle

Rotate vector B relative to vector A. The dot product measures how aligned the vectors are, not the angle itself.

Why this matters in shaders

For normalized vectors, A · B = cos(θ). A value of +1 means aligned, 0 means perpendicular, and −1 means opposite. Lambert diffuse lighting uses max(0, N · L).
